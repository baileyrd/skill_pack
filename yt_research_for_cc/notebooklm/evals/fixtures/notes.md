# Heat pumps in cold climates: notes

- Modern cold-climate air-source heat pumps keep a COP above 1.5 down to about -25 °C.
- Defrost cycles cost 5-10% of seasonal efficiency in humid cold climates.
- Ducted vs ductless: ductless mini-splits avoid duct losses (10-30% in unconditioned attics).
- Sizing: a Manual J load calculation beats rule-of-thumb tonnage; oversizing causes short cycling.
- Backup heat: resistance strips vs dual-fuel with a gas furnace; the switchover temperature matters.
